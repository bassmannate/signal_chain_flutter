Signal Chain is a pure Dart/Flutter patch editor, preset librarian, and live stage performance tool for guitar and bass multi-effects processors, supporting Zoom MS Plus series pedals (MS-60B+, MS-50G+, MS-70CDR+, MS-200D+), Zoom Four series (B1X FOUR, B1 FOUR), and the Line 6 Bass POD Pro over USB OTG MIDI.

Features:
* Real-time bidirectional parameter editing, rotary knob controls, hardware tuner toggle, and physical footswitch bypass synchronization.
* Right-to-left (OUT <- IN) pedalboard signal chain reordering, effect insertion, swapping, and removal.
* Hardware memory bank browsing, incremental sync, drag-and-drop Bank Manager, and batch slot restore.
* Live Performance Setlist Mode with ordered gig lists, Landscape Stage dashboard, and optional MIDI foot-controller stepping.
* Local preset library with Undo/Redo history, A/B tone compare, structured patch diff preview, and multi-format import/export (.json, .zptc, .zb1f, Symbiosis, .syx).
* 100% offline-capable with zero third-party pub.dev dependencies.
